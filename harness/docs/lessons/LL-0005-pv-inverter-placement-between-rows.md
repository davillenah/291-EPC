# LL-0005 · Where the string inverters go in a low-tilt ground plant

- **Discipline:** PV
- **Status:** proposed
- **Date and origin:** 2026-11-20, stress simulation `pvmax`
- **Resumen (es):** Con mesas de 5° los inversores de cadena no entran en el espacio entre filas (0,29 m); hay que decidir dónde van.

## Situation
Every ground plant of the simulation warned that the inverter pad (0.4 m deep) does not fit the gap between rows (0.29 m at 5° tilt) and the model drew the inverters there anyway.

## Cause
The layout places each inverter behind its first table. At low tilt the rows are close.

## Solution (to decide)
Options: at the end of the row, outside the field along a service road; on the structure behind the table when the manufacturer allows it; or a central station with string combiners. Each changes the cabling, the drawings and the BOQ. The owner chooses the standard.

## Evidence
Not tried: this is a proposal.

## Follow-up
PVX-05 (code), decision of the owner.
